# StoreLink — seller positioning (internal)

**One line:** StoreLink is **owned storefront + checkout + orders** with **marketplace discovery** and **Store Coins** for repeat buyers—not a generic “link in bio” stack and not a full business OS.

## Who it is for

- Sellers who already sell on social or WhatsApp and need a **trustworthy place to close** the sale.
- Brands that want **one canonical URL** plus **reach beyond their DMs**.
- Anyone who wants **commerce-first infrastructure** without commissioning a custom site.

## What it is not

- **Not** a marketplace-only cage where your brand disappears.
- **Not** accounting, legal, tax, or payroll software.
- **Not** a guarantee of rank, impressions, or sales (including on Diamond).

## Positioning vs common alternatives

| Alternative | StoreLink angle |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Instagram / TikTok only | Feeds are for attention; StoreLink is where **catalog, stock, and checkout** live on **your** link. |
| WhatsApp catalog + “send proof” | Same hustle, **structured orders** and less screenshot archaeology. |
| DIY WordPress / Wix build | **Hosted storefront**; we ship product improvements so you stay on listings and fulfilment. |
| Heavy “business OS” | We focus on **selling and getting paid**; we don’t replace your accountant. |

## What we should never imply

- Guaranteed customers or rank.
- “Zero fees everywhere” (payment providers have processing fees).
- That Diamond replaces good product, pricing, and fulfilment.

## Proof & voice

- Prefer **honest** FAQs and pricing copy over hype.
- Collect **real seller quotes** for the landing “social proof” strip as they become available.
